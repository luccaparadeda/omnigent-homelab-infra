# Code of conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and keep technical
disagreement focused on the work. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language,
doxxing, and publishing another person's credentials or private information are
not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation
when conduct threatens people, the community, or the security of the project.
Report conduct or security-sensitive concerns privately to the maintainers rather
than opening a public issue.
